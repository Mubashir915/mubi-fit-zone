Cardio That Fits Your Life

Walking, cycling, dancing, swimming, and running can all contribute to cardiovascular fitness. Choose an activity you enjoy so it is easier to keep going.

  • Begin at a comfortable pace
  • Increase duration gradually
  • Mix steady sessions with rest days
  • Stay hydrated and pay attention to your body
Plan Your Week

A Simple Cardio Ladder

Week 1

Easy Start

Try 15–20 minutes of comfortable walking.

Week 2

Build Rhythm

Add a few minutes if the first week feels manageable.

Week 3+

Find Your Flow

Keep a steady routine and adjust based on recovery.
