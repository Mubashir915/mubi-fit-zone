Program 03

Make Space to Reset.

Improve mobility, balance, and focus with mindful movement.

Move With Intention

Mobility and gentle stretching can complement strength and cardio training. Move slowly, breathe naturally, and never force a position.

  • Start with gentle neck and shoulder rolls
  • Try comfortable hip and ankle mobility
  • Use slow breathing to settle your pace
  • Modify movements to suit your body
Stretching should feel comfortable, not sharp or painful. Ask a qualified professional if you have an injury or movement concern.

10-Minute Reset

ActivityTime
Easy breathing1 minute
Shoulder and upper-back mobility2 minutes
Gentle hip mobility3 minutes
Calf and hamstring stretch2 minutes
Relaxed cool-down2 minutes
