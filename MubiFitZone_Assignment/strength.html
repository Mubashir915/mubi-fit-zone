Program 01

Get Stronger, One Rep at a Time.

Build a solid foundation with progressive strength training and smart technique.

Start With the Fundamentals

Strength training can help build muscle and support everyday movement. Start light, learn correct technique, and increase difficulty gradually.

  • Warm up for 5–10 minutes
  • Focus on controlled movement and form
  • Allow recovery between hard sessions
  • Track progress instead of chasing quick results
Beginner idea: try two full-body sessions per week, with rest days between them. Adjust to your health and experience.
