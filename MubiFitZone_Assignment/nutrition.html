Fuel Your Progress.

Simple nutrition habits can help support training, recovery, and daily energy.

Keep It Balanced

You don't need a complicated diet to start making helpful choices. Aim for variety, regular meals, enough fluids, and a pattern you can sustain.

  • Include vegetables and fruit regularly
  • Choose protein sources such as beans, eggs, fish, or chicken
  • Include grains and other energy-giving foods
  • Drink water throughout the day
  • Allow enough time for sleep and recovery

Example Balanced Plate
